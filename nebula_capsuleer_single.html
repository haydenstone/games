You are what you fly

Systems, not screenshots.

The ship is a small state machine: velocity has inertia, weapons have cooldown, hostile pilots maneuver and fire back, shields recharge after a delay, mining requires range, repairs cost credits, and docking turns ore into spendable currency.

This is still a prototype rather than a full MMO client, but it is deliberately built as a foundation that can grow into WebGL, multiplayer state sync, inventory, fittings, missions and an Ollama-driven agent layer.
